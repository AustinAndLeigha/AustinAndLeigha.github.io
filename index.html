🎅 HO! HO! HOW DOES THIS WORK?

It's basically a tiny Christmas registry. Pick something you like, claim it, and the site handles the rest.

1 Pick a gift
2 Claim it 🎁
3 Check your email
More details

Browse either Austin's list or Leigha's below. Once a normal gift is claimed, it disappears so somebody else doesn't accidentally buy the same thing.

Gift cards and other reusable ideas stay visible, because more than one person can get them.

We are not notified who buys what. It's completely anonymous.

When you claim something, you'll get a confirmation email telling you what you claimed.

For normal gifts, that email also has an Undo Claim link if you change your mind.

Please check your Spam folder if you don't see the email. If you accidentally enter the wrong email address, just let me know and I can reset the gift.

And no, I'm not selling your data 😂
